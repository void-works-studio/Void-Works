About

Void of Vanguards

A fast-paced top-down sci-fi space shooter where survival is your only mission.

Fight your way through endless waves of hostile forces, experiment with powerful weapons, unleash devastating active skills, and push your limits in an ever-escalating battle for survival.

Every run is an opportunity to improve your strategy, master new combinations, and set a new personal best.

Gameplay

Features

Fast-Paced Combat

Fluid, responsive gameplay built around quick decision-making. Outmaneuver relentless enemies, dodge incoming attacks, and rely on quick reflexes to survive the ever-growing chaos of the Void.

Distinct Space Ships

Pilot a variety of unique spacecraft, each with its own stat distribution, exclusive Active Skill, and Passive Skill. Choose the ship that best fits your strategy.

Unique Projectiles

Discover and master a variety of projectile types, each with its own strengths, weaknesses, and gameplay style. No two projectiles feel exactly alike.

Various Modulators

Enhance your projectiles with powerful Modulators that alter their behavior and unlock new combat possibilities. Experiment to create a build that matches your playstyle.

Powerful Active Skills

Turn the tide of battle with powerful abilities that can save you from impossible situations or maximize your offensive potential.

Mythical Attachments

Rare attachments that grant extraordinary enhancements beyond standard upgrades. Collect them to transform your combat capabilities and push your game to its true potential.

Global Leaderboards

Climb the global rankings by surviving longer, destroying more enemies, and achieving higher scores. Compete with players worldwide and prove you are the ultimate Vanguard.

Upgrade Cards

Shape every run with Upgrade Cards that appear throughout each run. Choose from a variety of powerful upgrades to adapt to new challenges and create unique builds.

Progressive Achievements

Take on a wide variety of challenges and unlock achievements by reaching milestones, mastering different mechanics, and proving your skills throughout your journey.

Detailed Statistics

Monitor your progress with detailed gameplay statistics — high scores, survival time, enemies destroyed, streaks, and other performance metrics to track your improvement over time.

Multiple Game Modes

Take on a variety of game modes, each with unique objectives, mechanics, and challenges. Every mode delivers a fresh way to experience the Void.

Easy to Learn, Hard to Master

Simple controls make it easy to jump in, while mastering every weapon and surviving the late game will put your skills to the real test.
